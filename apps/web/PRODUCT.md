# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Small-business support owners and leads. Non-technical; they upload FAQs and documents, tune a chatbot, check its answers and costs, and embed a widget on their site. Secondary: technical admins who manage members and settings.

## Product Purpose
A multi-tenant SaaS for building AI support chatbots that answer only from the customer's own knowledge (documents, websites) and cite their sources. Success: an owner trusts the answers enough to put the bot in front of customers.

## Positioning
Every answer is grounded and cited, refuses when evidence is missing, and shows its token cost. A generic chat tool cannot truthfully promise that.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Chatbots, knowledge (documents, websites), test chat with citations and cost, conversations, team and roles, widget install. Analytics and billing are not built yet and must be labelled as such, never faked.

## Brand Commitments
Name: Support Platform. Tone requested by the user: calm, trustworthy, light.

## Evidence on Hand
No customers, testimonials or benchmarks exist; do not invent any.

## Product Principles
1. Show the evidence: sources and cost are always visible next to an answer.
2. Calm over clever: the owner is checking work, not being entertained.
3. Honest states: unbuilt features are labelled, empty and error states say what to do next.
4. Accessible by default: keyboard, contrast and reduced motion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA.
