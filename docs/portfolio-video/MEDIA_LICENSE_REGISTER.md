## Downloaded for this production

From PyPI: the packages above into `tools/portfolio-video/.venv-tts` (about 1.3 GB). From Hugging
Face: `hexgrad/Kokoro-82M` (about 310 MB) and the Whisper `small.en` conversion used by
faster-whisper. From the spaCy model releases: `en_core_web_sm` (12.8 MB). All stay inside the
video workspace and are gitignored. The application's dependencies were not changed.
